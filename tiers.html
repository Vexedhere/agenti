Good to know

Simple answers before you choose.

Do coding builds use monthly credits too?

Yes. A coding build counts against the plan's daily build allowance and its monthly AI credits.

What happens when I use all my monthly credits?

The intended plan behavior is to pause additional AI requests until the monthly allowance resets or the account changes to a plan with more credits. Live account-based enforcement is still being connected.

Does MAX really have unlimited daily builds?

Yes—there is no separate daily build limit on MAX. Builds still consume the 100,000 monthly credits, so the monthly pool remains the limit.
